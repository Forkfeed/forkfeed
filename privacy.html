Privacy Policy

Your data, in plain English

ForkFeed is a social cooking app. This policy explains what we collect, why we need it, who helps us run the app, and the choices you have.

Last updated October 14, 2026

The short version

  • We collect what you give us to run the app: your account, what you cook and post, and the nutrition you choose to log.
  • You decide who sees each cook and recipe: Public, Followers or Private. Your meal logs, goals, grocery list and meal plan are always private.
  • No ads. We don't sell your data, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.
  • You can delete your account and everything in it at any time from Settings.

1. What we collect

Your account

Your email address and password (stored encrypted by our login provider), display name, username, bio and profile photo. When you sign up or reset your password we email you a one-time code.

What you create and share

  • Cooks: what you cooked, ingredients and amounts, steps, timing, servings, notes and photos.
  • Recipes: recipes you write, fork from others, save from your cooks or import from a web link.
  • Social activity: follows, likes, comments, forks and the cooks you share.
  • Messages: direct messages between you and people you follow who follow you back.

Nutrition and planning

Calories, macros, vitamins and minerals for your ingredients; meals you log as eaten and how many servings; your daily nutrition goals; your grocery list and weekly meal plan. This information can say something about your diet and health. We use it only to show it back to you, and it is never visible to other users.

Purchases

Whether you have ForkFeed Premium, which plan, and its renewal and expiry dates. Payments are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your card or bank details.

Device and safety information

  • A notification token for your device, if you allow push notifications.
  • Reports you file and accounts you block.
  • Basic technical logs (such as error messages and request times) kept by our hosting provider to keep the service running.
What we don't collect: your precise location, contacts, browsing history or advertising ID. We don't use analytics or ad trackers.

2. How we use it

  • To run ForkFeed: your profile, feed, Cook Mode, recipes and forks, comments, likes, messages, notifications, grocery list, meal planner and nutrition tracking.
  • To work out nutrition: ingredient names are matched against our ingredient catalog and the U.S. Department of Agriculture's FoodData Central database. Only the food name is sent, never who you are.
  • To suggest people to follow, based on public activity like how many cooks someone has shared.
  • To provide Premium features you've paid for.
  • To keep ForkFeed safe: reviewing reports, enforcing our Terms, and preventing spam and abuse.
  • To contact you about your account, such as sign-up codes, password resets and replies to support emails. We don't send marketing emails.

3. Who can see what

InformationWho can see it
Display name, username, bio, profile photo, follower countsEveryone on ForkFeed
Cooks and recipesYour choice for each one: Public (everyone), Followers (people who follow you) or Private (only you)
Comments and likesAnyone who can see the cook

When someone forks your recipe or cook, they get their own private copy that they can change. Forks show who they came from.

We may disclose information if the law requires it, or if it's needed to protect someone's safety or the security of ForkFeed.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as your account exists. You can delete individual cooks, recipes, comments, meal logs and grocery items at any time.

When you delete your account, we immediately delete your profile, photos, cooks, recipes, comments, likes, follows, messages, meal logs, goals, grocery list, meal plan, blocks, reports you filed and device tokens. Copies in encrypted backups are removed within 30 days. Purchase records may be kept by Google, Apple and RevenueCat where tax and accounting law requires it.

6. Your choices and rights

  • See and edit: view and change your profile and everything you've posted, right in the app.
  • Control visibility: set each cook and recipe to Public, Followers or Private.
  • Hide nutrition: turn on Nutrition-free mode in Settings to hide calories and macros. Logging what you eat is always optional.
  • Notifications: turn them off in your phone's settings at any time.
  • Block and report: block anyone, or report content, from the ⋮ menu.
  • Delete: Settings → Delete account, or follow these steps.

Depending on where you live, for example in the EU or UK (GDPR) or California (CCPA/CPRA), you may also have the right to access a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, move it to another service, or object to some processing. Email forkfeed.app@gmail.com and we'll respond within 30 days. We won't treat you differently for using these rights. Californians: we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined in California law.

7. Children

ForkFeed is not for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email forkfeed.app@gmail.com and we'll delete it.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest by our hosting provider. Database access rules make sure people can only see what they're allowed to, and passwords are never stored in plain text. No system is perfectly secure, but we work hard to protect your information and will tell you if a breach affects you.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For important changes, we'll also let you know in the app before they take effect.

10. Contact us

Questions or requests about your privacy: forkfeed.app@gmail.com
