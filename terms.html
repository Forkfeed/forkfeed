2. Your content and forks

You own your content: the cooks, recipes, photos, comments and messages you post.

So we can run ForkFeed, you give us a worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free license to store, display and share your content with the audience you choose (Public, Followers or Private), and to make the copies needed to run the app, like thumbnails and backups.

Forking: when you make a cook or recipe visible to others, you allow them to fork it, which saves their own copy that they can change and cook from. Forks keep a credit back to you. If you later delete your content or account, forks people already made remain theirs.

The license ends when you delete the content or your account, except for forks already made and copies in backups that expire within 30 days.

Only post content you have the right to share. You are responsible for what you post.

Don't misuse the app: no scraping, bots or automated accounts, no attacks on our systems, and no attempts to get around our security, rate limits or Premium features.
