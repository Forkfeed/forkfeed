Account deletion

Delete your ForkFeed account

You can permanently delete your account and data at any time, from the app or by email.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open ForkFeed and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap the gear icon to open Settings.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. Type DELETE and tap Delete forever.

Your account and data are deleted immediately and you're signed out.

Without the app

Email us from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my ForkFeed account". We'll confirm and delete it within 7 days.

Email a deletion request

What gets deleted

  • Your login, profile, username, bio and profile photo
  • All your cooks, recipes, forks and photos
  • Your comments, likes and follows
  • Your direct messages and conversations
  • Meal logs, nutrition goals, grocery list and meal plan
  • Blocks, reports you filed, and device notification tokens

What may be kept

  • Copies in encrypted backups, removed automatically within 30 days.
  • Forks other people made of your public or followers-only recipes before you deleted your account. Those copies belong to them and no longer link to you.
  • Purchase records kept by Google Play, the App Store and RevenueCat where tax law requires it.
Have Premium? Deleting your account doesn't cancel your subscription. Cancel it first in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions (or App Store settings).
